Privacy Notice

How Motus collects, uses and looks after your information when you contact us or book a conversation.

Last Updated · 16 November 2026

On This Page

Who Is Responsible

Motus is responsible for the personal information described in this notice. You can email us with a privacy question or request. You do not need to provide a business name or make a sales enquiry to do this.

hello@motusautomation.co.uk

What We Collect

  • Enquiries: your name, email address, optional business name, selected topic and the message you choose to send.
  • Bookings: your name, email address, chosen time, time zone and any information you provide about your business. For a phone call, we also collect your phone number. If you suggest another meeting location or platform, we receive that information too.
  • Calculator estimates: the task and figures you choose to include when you select Discuss My Savings. Using the calculator alone does not send us your figures.
  • Technical information: the website source label and limited connection or error information needed to operate and protect the connected service, such as an IP address and request time.

Please do not include passwords, confidential documents or personal details about your customers. You can choose not to contact or book with us. We need your name, email and message to respond through the enquiry form; your business name is optional.

Why We Use It

We use your information to understand and reply to your request, arrange a conversation, prepare a proposal and deliver work you agree with us. We also use limited information to prevent misuse and keep necessary business records.

Our legal grounds are legitimate interests in responding to business enquiries and keeping the service secure; taking steps you request before a contract or performing an agreed contract; and legal obligations where records must be kept. These grounds apply according to the purpose and circumstances.

Sending an enquiry or booking a conversation does not add you to a Motus marketing list. We do not sell your enquiry details or make solely automated decisions that accept or reject you as a client.

Where Your Details Go

  • Enquiries: the details you send pass through Cloudflare and Motus’s controlled n8n system to our Google Gmail business inbox. Only information needed to handle your request is included.
  • Bookings: Calendly handles the booking, with Google Calendar used to organise appointments and Google Meet used when you choose a video meeting. For another platform, we will agree the arrangements with you.
  • Website hosting and security: this website uses Cloudflare. Motus’s n8n service and its database are hosted with Contabo in Portsmouth, United Kingdom.
  • Service problems: limited error information may be recorded in Google Sheets to help investigate delivery problems.

Some providers, including Calendly, Google and Cloudflare, process information internationally, including outside the UK. Their privacy notices explain how they handle information and the transfer safeguards they use.

  • Calendly Privacy Notice (opens in a new tab)
  • Google Privacy Policy (opens in a new tab)
  • Cloudflare Privacy Policy (opens in a new tab)

How Long We Keep It

  • Enquiries that do not become a project: the existing Motus policy sets six months after the last meaningful contact.
  • Accepted projects: relevant contracts, invoices and important project decisions may be kept for up to six years after the relationship ends where needed for tax, legal or dispute records.
  • Booking details: kept for arranging the conversation and handling the resulting enquiry or project, using the relevant period above. Information held by providers for their own purposes follows their own notices.

Relevant records may be held in our inbox, Calendly and calendar. Information providers retain for their own purposes is covered by their privacy notices. You can contact Motus about the records we hold or request their deletion, subject to any lawful requirement to retain them.

Your Rights and Choices

Depending on the circumstances, you can ask to see your information, correct it, delete it, restrict its use or receive a portable copy. You can also object to processing based on legitimate interests. We will consider your request and explain if there is a lawful reason we need to keep or use particular information.

Email Motus to make a request. We may ask for enough information to confirm your identity before disclosing or changing personal information. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK data protection regulator.

hello@motusautomation.co.uk

  • Raise a Concern with the ICO (opens in a new tab)

Cookies and Tracking

These website pages do not add advertising or analytics tracking. Your enquiry draft and calculator entries are not saved to browser storage. Enquiry details are transmitted only when you select Send Enquiry. The calculator alone does not send your figures.

Calendly opens as a separate page rather than an embedded booking form. Its cookie choices and privacy notice apply there. Other linked services and demonstrations may have their own arrangements.

Cloudflare processes limited connection information to provide hosting and security. Its privacy notice explains that processing.
