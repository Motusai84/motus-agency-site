Can you help a business like mine?

Motus works with UK small businesses, including salons, barbers, trades, local services, property businesses and professional services. We’ll start by understanding what you need.

How long will my project take?

It depends on the work and the content or access needed. We’ll agree an expected timescale before starting.

Can I start small and add more later?

Yes. Start with a defined project and agree any additional features separately as your needs develop.

How do I find out what it will cost?

Tell us what you need. We’ll explain the proposed work, price, ongoing costs and support before you decide.

Can you work with tools I already use?

Where supported, yes. We’ll check the available connections and any limits before agreeing the project.

Can I see examples before deciding?

Yes. Try the existing demos to explore what we can build. Your own project’s features and scope are agreed separately.

What happens after launch?

You’ll receive a practical handover, with ownership, maintenance and any ongoing support explained.
